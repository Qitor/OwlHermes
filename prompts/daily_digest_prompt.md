# Daily Digest Prompt

Generate a concise Chinese daily digest for frontier AI risk intelligence.

Required sections:

1. 今日一句话总览
2. Top Signals
3. 政策/治理
4. Benchmark 与评估
5. 前沿实验室与模型发布
6. 新型风险
7. 风险框架与标准
8. 播客/访谈重点
9. 会议/论坛/峰会重点
10. arXiv/研究趋势
11. 需进一步深挖
12. 来源健康状态

For each important item include:

- what changed;
- why it matters;
- source link;
- confidence / review flag if needed.

Do not overstate podcast predictions or news commentary.
